Lake Latonka LLPOA – Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how users of the Lake Latonka LLPOA mobile app can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Lake Latonka LLPOA app account, send an email to:

llpoa.emergency@gmail.com

Use the subject line: LLPOA App Account Deletion Request

Please include the name and email address associated with your account so that we can identify the correct account.

What Will Be Deleted

Upon verification of your request, the account information and personal data associated with your LLPOA app account will be deleted from systems controlled by the Lake Latonka Property Owners Association, except for information that must be retained for legitimate administrative, security, legal, or recordkeeping purposes.

Data Retention

Information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud prevention, or association recordkeeping purposes may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Questions

Questions regarding account or data deletion may be sent to: llpoa.emergency@gmail.com
