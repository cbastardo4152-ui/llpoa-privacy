Privacy Policy

Lake Latonka Property Owners Association (LLPOA) Mobile App

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

Lake Latonka Property Owners Association ("LLPOA," "we," "our," or "us") respects the privacy of users of the Lake Latonka LLPOA mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains the information that may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information You Provide

Most features of the LLPOA mobile app can be accessed without providing personal information directly to the app. Certain features, including Lake Emergency/Security reporting, allow users to voluntarily provide information when submitting a report.

Information submitted through these features may include:

  • Name
  • Telephone number
  • A description of an emergency, security concern, or incident
  • A photograph selected or captured by the user
  • Location information when the user chooses to provide or use location functionality
  • Other information voluntarily included in a report

Location Information

The app may request access to device location for features where location is useful, such as identifying the location of an emergency or security report and generating map/location information. Location access is used only when permitted by the user and for the functionality requested by the user.

Photos and Camera

The app may allow users to attach a photograph to an emergency or security report. Access to the camera or photos is used only when the user chooses to capture or select an image for that purpose.

How Information Is Used

Information submitted through the app may be used to:

  • Receive and respond to emergency, safety, security, or community reports
  • Identify the location and nature of a reported incident
  • Communicate with the person submitting a report when appropriate
  • Provide requested app functionality
  • Maintain and improve the operation and security of the app

Third-Party Services and Links

The LLPOA app may provide links or access to external services, including the LLPOA member portal, LLPOA website, merchandise store, mapping services, and other third-party websites or services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and practices. LLPOA is not responsible for the privacy practices of independently operated third-party services.

Sharing of Information

LLPOA does not sell personal information collected through the app. Information submitted through reporting features may be provided to appropriate LLPOA representatives or other parties when reasonably necessary to respond to the report, protect persons or property, comply with applicable law, or address an emergency or security matter.

Data Retention and Security

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was submitted, administrative or operational needs, and applicable legal obligations. Reasonable measures are used to protect information; however, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The LLPOA app is intended as a community resource and is not specifically directed to children under 13. LLPOA does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13 through the app.

Your Choices

Users may decline optional device permissions such as location, camera, or photo access. Certain features requiring those permissions may not function without them.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

LLPOA may update this Privacy Policy as the application, its features, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
